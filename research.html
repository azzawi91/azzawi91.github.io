Research

Energy-efficient, secure wireless and IoT systems for healthcare

From what a wearable sensor should send, to how body-worn networks route it, to how edge AI stays reliable after deployment: an overview of research themes, current manuscripts, and publications.

01

Research Themes

Semantic communication

Task-oriented coding for wearable sensing

Sending the decision-relevant content of a signal instead of the raw samples, to save energy and airtime on battery-powered sensors.

Post-quantum security

Security for constrained devices

Quantum-resistant session security and authenticated encryption sized for wearables and IoT nodes.

Body area networks

Bio-inspired & learning-based routing

Reliable, energy-aware routing for healthcare WBANs on moving patients, with formal analysis and reproducible simulation.

Edge AI reliability

Drift monitoring for deployed models

Lightweight signals that track and anticipate the degradation of frozen machine-learning models on IoT edge devices.

Federated learning

Secure aggregation & availability

How adversaries exploit availability in privacy-preserving federated learning, and metadata-only defenses.

IoT security & privacy

Authentication & privacy-preserving mining

Lightweight IoT authentication, secure traffic exchange, intrusion and anomaly detection, and privacy-preserving data mining.
